Disconnect QuickBooks Online (QBO)

Effective date: January 8, 2025

This page explains how to disconnect QuickBooks Online (“QBO”) from BeeTech Services. BeeTech is operated by The Bee Group LLC, doing business as BeeTech.

Disconnect from BeeTech

  1. Sign in to BeeTech.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Integrations → QuickBooks Online.
  3. Select Disconnect and confirm.

Disconnect from Intuit

You can also revoke BeeTech’s access from your Intuit account / QuickBooks connected apps area. The exact navigation may vary based on Intuit’s UI.

What happens after disconnect

  • BeeTech will stop requesting new data from QBO.
  • Any automated synchronization and write operations will be disabled.
  • Previously imported data may remain in BeeTech according to your agreement and our Privacy Policy, unless you request deletion where applicable.
