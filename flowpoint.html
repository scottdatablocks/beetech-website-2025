DTC Cooling System Sizing: Role-Play Scenarios

This guide outlines the required dialogue flow for engineers to gather inputs for the FlowPoint Automated Sizing and Sourcing AI Tool, ensuring all critical thermal and hydraulic data points are acquired.

Scenario 1: Engineer (FlowPoint) to Customer (Data Center Ops)

Goal: Define QTotal, ΔT, Rack Count, and initial Fluid Type for the FlowPoint tool's Phase 1 Inputs.

The engineer should gather: Max IT Load per rack, Number of racks, Target supply/return temperatures (to calculate Delta T), and Coolant type (water or glycol mix).

Scenario 2: Engineer to Cold Plate Vendor (Hydraulic Performance)

Goal: Obtain the single most critical hydraulic input: the ΔP required across the cold plates at the calculated nodal flow rate.

Request the cold plate pressure drop specification at nominal flow, and gather K-factor data for manifold connectors and tubing bundles.

Scenario 3: Engineer to QDC/Manifold Supplier (Sourcing Metrics)

Goal: Define the Cv minimum, check material compatibility, and finalize the BOM quantities for accessories.

Specify QDC requirements including Cv target, hose size, and material compatibility. Calculate total QDC quantity including 10% spares.

Process Summary: By following this precise flow, the engineer ensures every field in the FlowPoint tool is populated with validated data, transitioning directly from calculation to a fully quantified Bill of Materials (BOM).
