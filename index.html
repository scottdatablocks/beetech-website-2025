📋 Guides & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cooling infrastructure do you source?

We source a comprehensive range of cooling infrastructure including liquid cooling components, rear-door heat exchangers, in-row cooling units, CRAC/CRAH systems, CDUs, manifolds, and all associated peripherals for modern data center deployments.

How do you help with OT platform selection?

Our technology consulting practice provides independent advisory services to evaluate, select, and implement operational technology platforms. We manage the entire process from requirements gathering through vendor selection, negotiation, and deployment oversight.

What makes BeeTech different from other consultants?

We combine deep technical expertise with hands-on execution. Unlike traditional consultants who deliver reports, we provide on-site program management, procurement support, and ongoing oversight to ensure successful project delivery and measurable cost savings.

How quickly can you start on a new project?

We can typically begin engagement within 1-2 weeks of initial consultation. For urgent infrastructure needs or time-sensitive procurement, we can mobilize even faster.
